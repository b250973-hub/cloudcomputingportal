Overview

Why Cloud Computing?

Cloud Computing allows individuals and companies to use computing resources like servers, storage and software over the internet, instead of buying and maintaining their own hardware. This reduces cost, increases flexibility, and allows access to data from anywhere, anytime.

  • Pay only for what you use
  • Access data from anywhere
  • Easy to scale up or down
  • Automatic backup and maintenance
